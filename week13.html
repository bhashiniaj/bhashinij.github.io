Week 13: Inductor Winding Calculation & Schematic Refinement

Phase: Phase 3: Hardware Build

Status: ✅ Completed

What We Did This Week

  • Confirmed the toroidal ferrite core for the boost inductor (L1), as specified by our supervisor: MS-130060-2 (33.8 x 19.3 x 11.6mm), AL = 61nH/N².
  • Calculated the number of turns needed to reach our target inductance of 2000µH using N = √(L / AL), giving N ≈ 181 turns.
  • Drew a simple, hand-drawn circuit diagram of the boost converter for quick reference during winding and assembly.
  • Documented the full calculation and core specifications in this week's report (PDF, below).

Week 13 Report

Inductor winding calculation, toroidal core specs, and schematic refinement for the STM32 Bluepill switch.

Challenges & Solutions

We ran into problems when switching the control design from Arduino Uno to STM32 Bluepill — pin assignments, logic levels, and gate driver supply connections all had to be reworked, and a few wiring mistakes (like the VDD/VCC voltage mix-up on the IR2110) only showed up after redrawing the schematic for STM32.

Plan for Next Week

Finalize the PCB with these connections to move forward with making the prototype.
